About

My recent project work focuses on applying large language models and retrieval-augmented generation to real documents and data — from a financial-document question-answering system to an AI-assisted evaluation framework for corporate disclosures.

That habit carries directly into how I approach analytics and AI today.

I'm seeking opportunities in AI engineering, data analytics, and business analysis — and open to consulting as well — where I can apply quantitative methods and machine learning to solve real business problems.
