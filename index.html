About

I like working where AI, analytics, and business problems meet. Most of what I build starts with unstructured, real-world data — filings, reviews, disclosures — and ends with something concrete enough to act on.

I earned my M.S. in Business Analytics from UC Irvine, where my work moved steadily toward applied AI: retrieval-augmented question answering over financial filings, NLP and sentiment analysis on customer reviews, RAG-based scoring of corporate disclosures, and machine-learning models on satellite data. The common thread is RAG, NLP, LLMs, and analytics applied to information that doesn't come pre-structured.

Before graduate school I worked in audit at KPMG Huazhen and marketing at Lenovo. That left me with a business-first instinct that still shapes how I work: the model matters, but so does the evidence behind it, the context around it, and the decision it's meant to support.

I'm looking for applied-AI and analytics roles where I can build practical systems around real data — and stay close to the business problems they exist to solve.
