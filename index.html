About

I'm a Master of Science in Business Analytics graduate from UC Irvine, where my work increasingly focused on applied machine learning, LLM applications, and data-driven decision support.

My recent work spans financial document intelligence, e-commerce review analysis, corporate purpose scoring, and remote sensing — combining analytics, machine learning, and LLMs to turn complex real-world data into useful insights.

Earlier in my career, I worked as an Audit Assistant at KPMG Huazhen LLP and a Marketing Assistant at Lenovo Group, where I developed a habit of working carefully with evidence — verifying sources and tracing conclusions back to the underlying data. That habit carries directly into how I approach analytics and AI today.

I'm seeking opportunities at the intersection of applied AI, analytics, and business problem solving — where I can build practical AI and analytics solutions around real-world data and decisions.
