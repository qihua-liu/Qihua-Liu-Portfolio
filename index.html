About

I'm a Master of Science in Business Analytics graduate from UC Irvine, with a focus on applied machine learning, LLM applications, and data systems.

My recent project work focuses on applying large language models and retrieval-augmented generation to real documents and data — from a financial-document question-answering system to an AI-assisted evaluation framework for corporate disclosures.

Earlier in my career, I worked as an Audit Assistant at KPMG Huazhen LLP and a Marketing Assistant at Lenovo Group, where I developed a habit of working carefully with evidence — verifying sources and tracing conclusions back to the underlying data. That habit carries directly into how I approach analytics and AI today.

I'm seeking opportunities in AI engineering, data analytics, and business analysis — and open to consulting as well — where I can apply quantitative methods and machine learning to solve real business problems.
