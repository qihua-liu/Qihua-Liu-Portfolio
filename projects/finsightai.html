FinSightAI — Financial Document Intelligence Copilot

RAG LLM Q&A Risk Classification FAISS Gemini Streamlit
View on GitHub

The Problem

SEC filings like 10-Ks run well over 100 pages, with financial, operational, and risk information scattered across unstructured text. Reading them one at a time doesn't scale when you're tracking dozens of companies, and the professional tools that solve this cost $10K+ per year.

Approach

FinSightAI answers plain-English questions about filings with citations, compares companies, tracks multi-year trends, and classifies risk. I built the question-answering and risk-classification layer — the part that turns retrieved passages into a trustworthy answer:

  • Grounded Q&A — generates answers only from retrieved SEC passages, constrained to what the evidence actually supports
  • Citation validation — every answer traces back to a specific filing and section rather than an unverifiable claim
  • Risk classification — labels retrieved content into liquidity, market, regulatory, and operational risk categories
  • Retrieval pipeline — SEC EDGAR ingestion, ~1,200-character chunks, MiniLM embeddings indexed in FAISS, and section-aware top-K search feeding the answer layer

Results

  • Retrieval-quality evaluation: Recall@5 and MRR of 0.68, with a 63% section-hit rate — the numbers that make the citations trustworthy
  • Grounded answers with working citations across single-company Q&A, multi-company comparison, and 5-year trend views
  • Automatic risk labels that hold up across very different filing styles — a bank's risk section reads nothing like a tech company's

Reflections

The hard part of an LLM-over-documents system isn't getting an answer — it's keeping that answer constrained to the retrieved evidence and traceable to its source. The citation-validation and risk-classification layer is where most of that work lives.
